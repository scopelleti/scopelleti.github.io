Privacy Policy

Agent Scope for Sparked · scope.inc · Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Overview

scope.inc ("we", "us", or "our") operates Agent Scope for Sparked ("the Service"), a private tool that manages Sparked's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the Service accesses and how it is used.

2. Data We Access

With Sparked's authorization through Google sign-in, the Service accesses only Sparked's own YouTube account:

  • Basic channel information, used only to confirm the correct channel is connected
  • Permission to upload videos to the channel

3. How We Use Data

  • Upload Sparked's videos to its YouTube channel on a schedule

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share this data with anyone. Data is never shared with other scope.inc clients.

5. Google API Services

Agent Scope's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The Service uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Storage and Retention

Access tokens and channel data are stored on a secured computer operated by scope.inc. They are deleted when Sparked ends the service or revokes access.

7. Revoking Access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. To request deletion of stored data, contact us below.
