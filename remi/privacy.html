Privacy Policy

Agent Scope for Maison ORemi · scope.inc · Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Overview

scope.inc ("we", "us", or "our") operates Agent Scope for Maison ORemi ("the Service"), a private tool that manages Maison ORemi's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the Service accesses and how it is used.

2. Data We Access

With Maison ORemi's authorization through Google sign-in, the Service accesses only Maison ORemi's own YouTube account:

  • Channel information and the list of the channel's videos
  • Video analytics (views, likes, watch statistics)
  • Comments posted on the channel's videos
  • Permission to upload videos and post comment replies on the channel

3. How We Use Data

  • Upload Maison ORemi's videos to its YouTube channel on a schedule
  • Track video performance to improve titles and posting decisions
  • Draft replies to comments; replies are posted only after manual approval

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share this data with anyone. Data is never shared with other scope.inc clients. Short excerpts such as video titles and comment text may be processed by AI services solely to generate titles and reply drafts for this channel.

5. Google API Services

Agent Scope's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The Service uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Storage and Retention

Access tokens and channel data are stored on a secured computer operated by scope.inc. They are deleted when Maison ORemi ends the service or revokes access.

7. Revoking Access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. To request deletion of stored data, contact us below.
